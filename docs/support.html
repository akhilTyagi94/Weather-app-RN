Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the app need my location?
WeatherNow uses your location to show current weather for where you are, and (if you allow background location) to check for severe weather in your area even when the app is closed.

Can I use the app without sharing my location?
Yes. If you decline location access, you can search for any city manually using the search bar.

What is the Wellness tab?
It gives general clothing, hydration, nutrition, and exercise suggestions based on today's weather. It's general wellness guidance only, not medical advice.

How do I stop severe weather alerts?
You can disable notifications and/or location access for WeatherNow at any time in your device's Settings.
