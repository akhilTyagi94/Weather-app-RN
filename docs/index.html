Privacy Policy for WeatherNow

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the WeatherNow mobile app ("WeatherNow", "the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. WeatherNow does not require you to create an account. The app routes weather requests through a lightweight proxy server we operate; that server does not log, store, or retain any of your data — it only forwards your request to our weather data provider and returns the response.

Information We Collect

Location Data

WeatherNow uses your device's location to show current weather conditions and, if you enable severe weather alerts, to check for severe weather in your area while the app is in the background.

  • Your coordinates are sent to our proxy server, which forwards them to our weather data provider, OpenWeatherMap, solely to retrieve the weather for your location. Our proxy server does not log or store your coordinates.
  • We do not store your location history on any server we control.
  • Background location access is used only to periodically check weather conditions for severe weather alerts, even when the app is closed. You can deny or revoke this at any time in your device's Settings.

Favorites and Preferences

Locations you save as favorites and your app preferences are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party, and are deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Advertising Data

WeatherNow displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To do this:

  • On iOS, we request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before using the advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. You can decline this, and you'll still see ads, just not personalized to you.
  • If you're in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you'll see a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) asking how you'd like ads to be personalized, in line with GDPR requirements.
  • Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and app usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data collection policy.

Notifications

Severe weather alerts are delivered as local notifications generated on your device. We do not use a push notification server, and no notification content is sent to us.

How We Use Information

  • To show you current and forecast weather for your location.
  • To generate general wellness and lifestyle suggestions (clothing, hydration, nutrition, exercise, precautions) based on current weather conditions. These suggestions are calculated on your device from the weather data described above — no additional data is collected for this feature.
  • To alert you to severe weather conditions in your area.
  • To display advertising that helps keep the app free.

Third-Party Services

WeatherNow relies on the following third parties, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • OpenWeatherMap — weather data, using coordinates you provide via device location.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties except as described above, to provide the app's core functionality (weather data) and to serve ads.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke location permissions (including background location) at any time in your device's Settings. The app will simply be unable to show local weather or severe weather alerts.
  • You can decline App Tracking Transparency on iOS, or reset/limit ad tracking in your device's ad settings.
  • You can adjust or withdraw ad personalization consent at any time by reopening the consent form from the app's settings, where available, or through your device's privacy settings.
  • You can disable notifications for WeatherNow at any time in your device's Settings.

Children's Privacy

WeatherNow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Security

Location and preference data used by the app stays on your device, or passes through our proxy server (which does not store it) to the third parties described above, all over encrypted connections. We do not maintain a server that stores your personal data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
